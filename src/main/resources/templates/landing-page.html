Our History

A mission that began with hope and continues through generations

The Sisters of Mary Schools were founded as part of the mission of the Religious Congregation of the Sisters of Mary, established by Venerable Aloysius Schwartz in Busan, South Korea. The mission expanded to the Philippines and grew into institutions known for education, formation, and service to the poor.

Rooted in service and educational compassion

From its earliest years, the school has pursued an integrated form of education that addresses academic development, moral formation, and practical support. This legacy continues today in communities where students are empowered to build better futures.
