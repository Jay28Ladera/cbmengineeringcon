Import & Backup

Bring existing records in from Excel or CSV instead of typing them, and download a full copy of the data.

Import

  1. Download the template and fill it in Excel (keep the header row; columns can be in any order, and unused ones can be deleted).
  2. Save it as Excel (.xlsx) or CSV and upload it here.
  3. Check the preview: nothing is saved until you confirm.

Opening quantities go into stock at the unit cost given (net of VAT). Afterwards, bring their value into the books in Accounting › Opening balances › inventory.

Rate type is DAILY or MONTHLY; contribution cutoff FIRST (26–10), SECOND (11–25) or BOTH; tax status REGULAR, MWE or NONE. Blank cells leave existing values as they are.

Use the balances at the start date from your old books. Cash and bank balances can go here too (or use Accounting › Opening balances). Customer and supplier balances are posted as totals; enter open invoices and bills in Client Billing and Payables if you need them per client or supplier.

VAT registered and Subcontractor: Yes / No. EWT rate in % (1, 2, 5…). Payee type: Corporation or Individual.

Backup

A ZIP with every table as a CSV, plus a restore.sql to load it into a new database. Opening a CSV in Excel lets you look at any record.
