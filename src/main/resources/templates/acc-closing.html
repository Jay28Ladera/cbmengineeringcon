Accounting

Closing

Close a month once it is reviewed, so nothing can be posted into it by mistake. At year end, close the year: income and expenses go to retained earnings and the new year starts at zero.

Closed through

Before closing a month: post the payroll, record the month's bills and petty cash, check the trial balance, and reconcile the bank balances.
