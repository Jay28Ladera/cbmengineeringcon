Pay rules

The multipliers the pay sheet uses. The starting values are the ones in the Excel payroll. Locked payrolls keep their figures when these change.

01Overtime, Sundays and holidays

Added per day worked
100% = double pay
Of rate/hr, per ND hour

02Lates

1.3 = minute rate plus 30%
Subtracted from each day's late minutes. Use 0 if you already type minutes after grace.

03Work schedule (for biometric logs)

Used to turn punches into REG hours, OT and late minutes. Lates count from the shift start; REG is counted in half-hours within the shift, less the break; leaving early reduces REG.

30 = 6:00–6:29 PM out counts 1 hr OT

04Contributions and withholding tax

Taxable pay = gross − 13th month / bonuses − lates and absences − employee SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG. Minimum wage earners and employees set to "don't withhold" are skipped.
SSS 15% of the salary credit (5% employee), PhilHealth 5% (half each), Pag-IBIG 2% each up to ₱10,000. Fixed amounts on the employee still win.

Employer shares are worked out from the employee shares and posted with the payroll in Accounting.

05Rates and payslips

Rate/hr = rate/day ÷ this
Daily rate of monthly staff with none set = monthly × 12 ÷ this
